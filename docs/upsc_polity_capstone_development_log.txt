============================================================
6. FACT VERIFICATION
============================================================

FactVerifier outputs:
SUPPORTED / CONTRADICTED / INSUFFICIENT.

Principles:
- Only supplied evidence
- No outside knowledge
- Complete evidence required for SUPPORTED
- Explicit conflict required for CONTRADICTED
- INSUFFICIENT when evidence is incomplete

Negative claim handling was added using:
src/verification/negative_claim_checker.py

Purpose:
Handle claims such as:
"There is no mention of the word 'political party'..."

Test:
The term "political party" was found in the Constitution corpus.
Q58 Statement II was therefore correctly identified as CONTRADICTED.

============================================================
8. Q58 DETAILED FINDING
============================================================

Statement I contains two separate factual components:

1. President decides relevant parliamentary disqualification questions and the
   decision is final.
2. President acts in accordance with Council of Ministers' advice.

Initial ClaimRetriever results:
- Constitution p.77: Article 103
- Constitution p.378: Tenth Schedule
- Constitution p.117: Article 192

The missing Council of Ministers evidence was NOT a corpus limitation.

A deterministic grep of chunks.jsonl found Article 74 on Constitution p.65:
Council of Ministers aids and advises the President, and the President acts in
accordance with such advice.

Therefore the problem is:

Original compound claim
-> single retrieval query
-> one required constitutional provision is missed

This is a multi-hop / compound-claim retrieval problem.

Important:
Do not force the verifier to mark the claim SUPPORTED merely because the
official PYQ answer is known.

============================================================
9. CURRENT ARCHITECTURAL LESSON
============================================================

Current verification:
Claim
-> independent retrieval
-> FactVerifier

Observed limitation:
A compound claim may require multiple constitutional provisions.

Planned architecture:
Compound claim
-> decomposition into atomic subclaims
-> independent retrieval for each subclaim
-> verification of each subclaim
-> aggregate result

Example for Q58:
Subclaim 1:
Relevant parliamentary disqualification questions are decided by the President
and the decision is final.
Evidence: Article 103, p.77.

Subclaim 2:
The President acts in accordance with the advice of the Council of Ministers.
Evidence: Article 74, p.65.

Aggregation:
- all supported -> SUPPORTED
- any explicit contradiction -> CONTRADICTED
- otherwise -> INSUFFICIENT

============================================================
10. IMPORTANT FAILURE-ANALYSIS PRINCIPLES
============================================================

Do not:
- weaken verification just to increase the score
- use official answers to influence verification
- treat missing evidence as contradiction
- treat failure to retrieve a term as proof of absence
- invent evidence
- silently add outside sources
- generalize benchmark results

Do:
- preserve baseline results
- separate retrieval, verification, reasoning and mapping failures
- use deterministic parsing when reproducibility matters
- use independent retrieval for verification
- record every modification and test
